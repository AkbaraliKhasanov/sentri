Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

HomeSupport

Your privacy matters. The short version: scanning happens entirely on your device, and we collect no personal data.

1. Data We Collect

Nothing. Sentri has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs and no accounts. Scan results never leave your device.

2. Local Network

Sentri uses local network access only to discover devices on the Wi-Fi you are connected to. It does not send this information anywhere.

3. Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to list nearby devices and their signal strength on your screen. No Bluetooth data is stored or transmitted.

4. Sensors & Camera

Magnetometer readings and the camera lens finder run live on your device and are never recorded, stored or shared.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

6. Local Storage

A small amount of data (onboarding status, preferences) stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.
