Only finding a few devices?

Sentri can only see devices on the same Wi-Fi as your iPhone. Some routers enable "client isolation" (common on guest networks), which blocks devices from seeing each other. Modern phones also use randomized private Wi-Fi addresses, so they may appear as "Private device". This is normal and outside the app's control.

Sentri Pro

Sentri Pro is an auto-renewable monthly subscription that unlocks all scanning tools. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings ▸ Apple Account ▸ Subscriptions.

Note

Sentri is an informational tool and cannot guarantee detection of every device. Results depend on your network, hardware and environment.
