What Sentri does

Sentri turns your iPhone into a pocket security scanner. Scan your Wi-Fi, sweep nearby Bluetooth signals, and use the magnetometer and lens finder to spot hidden electronics — all privately, on your device.

Private by design

No accounts, no tracking, no data collection. Your scan results never leave your iPhone.
